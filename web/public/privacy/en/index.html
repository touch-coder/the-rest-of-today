Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

The Rest of Today is a personal sleep journal. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Your data stays on your device

Sleep entries, goals, and settings are stored on your device. The web version uses browser storage; the mobile app uses a local database. The app does not upload this data to our servers and does not provide accounts or cloud synchronization.

Backups and sharing

When you export or share a backup, the operating system's file and sharing features handle it. You choose where a file is saved and who receives it. JSON files can migrate and restore data; CSV files are for viewing details.

Reminders

You may enable local device reminders. They are scheduled on your device and are not sent through a remote notification service. You can turn them off in app or system settings.

Third parties and analytics

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, online AI, or health platform integrations. If you access the web version through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process ordinary web access logs under its policies. These logs do not contain sleep records uploaded by the app.

Deletion and migration

You can clear local records in the app. Uninstalling the app, clearing browser data, or changing devices may make data without a backup unrecoverable. Export a JSON backup before migrating.

Policy updates

Updates will be published on this page. This version has no feature that collects or uploads sleep data.
